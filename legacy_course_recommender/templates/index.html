The Science Under The Hood

How Our Recommendation Engine Works

We combine Natural Language Processing with vector mathematics to calculate genuine topical relevance without black boxes or external paid APIs.

1. Profile Synthesis

Collects your target interests, skill level, desired difficulty, and custom learning aspirations.

2. TF-IDF Vectors

Builds term frequency & inverse document frequency matrices for combined titles, descriptions, and syllabus skills.

3. Cosine Similarity

Calculates cosine angle distance between user query vector and all course document vectors in multidimensional space.

4. Ranked Recommendations

Applies platform and minimum rating constraints, then outputs top matching courses with transparent relevance scores.
